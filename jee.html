FAQ

JEE counselling, explained

Do I need JEE Advanced to get counselling help?
No. JEE Main qualifiers can target NITs, IIITs and GFTIs through JoSAA and CSAB, and we help with those just as closely.
What is freeze, float and slide?
After a seat is allotted, freeze accepts it and exits further rounds, float keeps you eligible for better institutes, and slide keeps you eligible for better branches within the same institute. Your mentor helps you choose each round.
Can you help if I'm still in Class 11?
Yes. Our Prep Mentorship plan builds strong fundamentals, a sustainable routine and a timeline for boards and JEE.
