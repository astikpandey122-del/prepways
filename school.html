FAQ

For school leaders

Is there a minimum school size to partner?
No. We work with schools of every size and tailor programs to your student strength and budget.
Will foundation batches disturb the regular timetable?
No. Batches are planned around your timetable — as zero periods, after-school sessions or weekend classes.
How does the ₹99 trial work?
You pay ₹99 and set up AutoPay to use the full school management tool for 7 days. We remind you at least 24 hours before the trial ends. If you don't cancel, ₹7,000 is charged automatically for the next 12 months.
How do we cancel AutoPay?
Cancel from your UPI app's AutoPay section or your card's mandate settings, or email prepways23@gmail.com. Cancel before the trial ends to avoid the ₹7,000 charge. Payments already made are non-refundable, as per our Terms.
Can we start with only a seminar?
Yes. Many schools begin with a career counselling seminar and expand to foundation programs and dashboards later.
