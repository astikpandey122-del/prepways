FAQ

Questions, answered

Who can join PrepWays counselling?
Students who have appeared in or qualified JEE Main, JEE Advanced or NEET UG — and aspirants in Class 11, 12 or a drop year who want structured preparation guidance.
Are the mentors really from IITs and medical colleges?
Yes. JEE mentors are IIT graduates and NEET mentors are MBBS students from leading medical colleges. Each mentor is verified before they are assigned to students.
Do you guarantee a seat?
No honest counsellor can guarantee a seat. We guarantee a well-researched strategy, accurate choice filling and support in every round so you get the best seat your rank allows.
How does school enrollment work?
Schools fill the enrollment form, we schedule a needs-assessment call, then design a program (seminars, foundation batches, dashboards) and launch it on campus.
